How it works

  1. Scan. Enter the URLs of your key screens (sign-in, dashboard, main workflows). An automated engine checks them against WCAG 2.2 A/AA.
  2. Check. Automated tools catch only part of the problems. For every remaining criterion you get short, plain-English test steps (keyboard, screen reader, zoom) and record what you found.
  3. Export. Download a completed VPAT 2.5 report (WCAG, Section 508, or International edition) as a Word document, with conservative wording drafted from your results.

Who it's for

Small B2B software teams without an accessibility specialist, who need a credible report for an enterprise, university or government buyer.

What it is not

  • Not a certification. There is no official VPAT certification.
  • Not legal advice, and not a guarantee of compliance with the ADA, Section 508 or the European Accessibility Act.
  • Not an overlay or widget. Nothing is injected into your site.
  • It will never mark something "Supports" unless you tested it and recorded that result.
