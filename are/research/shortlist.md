# Top 5 opportunities (from `are.py score`, exclusions removed)

Scenario figures are **hypotheses at month 6 after launch, not forecasts**. Each one states the volumes it assumes so it can be falsified. Net = revenue − Stripe (~2.9% + $0.30/txn, US standard) − hosting − AI usage. Founder time is excluded.

## 1. O01 — ACR/VPAT self-assessment kit for small B2B SaaS — 3.70 ★ selected
**Evidence:** Consultants charge ~$550–$4,450+ per VPAT [S10]. VPATs gate enterprise and government deals [S13]. A low-end generator exists at about $49 [S11]. A free GSA editor and free ITI template exist [S12].
**Main risks:**
- The free substitutes are real competition.
- The search results page may be dominated by consultancies.
- Buyers may discount self-produced reports.
- A misleading ACR creates liability, so the product must be honest by design.

| Scenario | Assumption | Monthly net |
|---|---|---|
| Downside | Pre-sale fails (< 3 orders) → killed after 21 days | −$115 one-time (capped) |
| Base | 8 reports × $149 (≈ 800 qualified visitors at 1%) | ≈ $1,110 |
| Upside | 35 reports × $149 + 20 refresh subs × $29 | ≈ $5,500 |

**Scenarios:** Withheld. More research is needed before any numbers are credible. It's listed only because the score qualifies it.

## Why O01 over O02/O03 despite a near-tie
1. **It can be validated before building.** A paid pre-order tests demand now. O02 and O03 can only be tested in their marketplaces after the app is built and approved, which would break the "distribution before development" rule.
2. **Fewest customers needed.** Seven sales a month reaches ~$1k. O02 needs about 70 subscribers for the same result.
3. **An urgent buying moment with budget behind it.** A blocked deal is worth far more than $149.
4. **Reuse.** O01 builds the same scanning engine (axe-core + headless browser) that O02 would need. That makes O02 the natural fallback if O01 fails, and the adjacent product if it succeeds.

- Lead-gen data (violates the no-prospecting rule).
