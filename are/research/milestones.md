## Evidence required before advancing

| Milestone | Gate |
|---|---|
| M1 First verified paying customer | One payment settled in the payment provider (or marketplace) and recorded verified=yes in the ledger; no refund within 14 days. |
| M2 $1,000 monthly owner profit | ≥ 3 consecutive months of measured logo churn ≤ model churn; ≥ 50% of new customers from the planned organic channel; support ≤ 2 h/week. |
| M3 $5,000 monthly owner profit | Organic channel delivers the required gross adds for 3 straight months without paid spend; measured CAC ≤ 1/3 of measured LTV; refunds < 3% of revenue. |
| M4 $10,000 monthly owner profit | Second acquisition channel proven (≥ 20% of adds); annual-plan share ≥ 20%; automated onboarding with ≤ 10% of trials needing human help. |
| M5 $25,000 monthly owner profit | Net revenue retention ≥ 100% (expansion offsets churn); part-time support contractor in place with documented SOPs; founder ≤ 7 h/week. |
| M6 $50,000 monthly owner profit | 12 months of verified MRR growth; customer concentration: no customer > 5% of MRR; reserve ≥ 3 months of fixed costs. |
