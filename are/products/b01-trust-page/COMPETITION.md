## What this means
- **The free trust page is not a moat.** Several vendors give one away. Our differences:
  - no account;
  - no data stored by us (the page lives in the link);
  - live in about 10 minutes;
  - a downloadable answer bank.

  These help adoption but are easy to copy.
- **The $49/mo paid tier competes head-on** with Attestly (from $49) and ResponseHub (~$50), and VendorSafe charges per use ($29 per questionnaire). The Stage 2 waitlist test must show buyers prefer ours. If paid interest is weak, the right move is to stop, not to cut the price.
