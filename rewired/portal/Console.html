Learning log

Every scan you send updates the template. This log records what changed and which case caused it.

Case 001: WAVi baseline, 48-year-old male, 10/31/2026

  • Added a pre-scan state step. The printout's sleep, time since meal and change fields were N/A. Sleep loss is known to lengthen P300 latency and shrink amplitude, so the report now names any missing state field.
  • Added a data-quality gate. Four of the eight non-alpha metrics were flagged low yield, reaction time was N/A, and frontal sites were <0 µV, probably from blinks. The report now grades overall confidence and recommends a standardized retest when confidence is low.
  • Added a site-consistency check. Good central-parietal sites ranged from 328 to 492 ms (Cz was the late outlier against P3/Pz/P4 at about 330 ms). The engine now applies WAVi's own consistency rule (at least 40% of good C-P pairs within 75 ms) and reports the spread.
  • Added a reaction-time compliance prompt. WAVi reports N/A below 15 clicks. The technician now confirms clicking instructions at setup.
  • Added alpha-rhythm strengths to the snapshot. Peak alpha of 11.0 Hz in every region and a normal frontal/occipital ratio are clean findings worth telling the client. The report now opens with them so it doesn't read as deficits only.

Cases 002–005: Drive archive (WAVi printout + 3 neurologist summaries, de-identified)

  • Corrected a rule: P300 voltage above range is no longer scored as a strength. In one case (44-year-old male, 22.7 µV), the neurologist listed it among the abnormal findings. It now reads "Above range: interpret clinically", with an artifact check first. Delay below range is handled the same way.
  • Added a near-500 ms delay flag. One case (30-year-old female) had a 496 ms delay, read as abnormally prolonged. The engine now flags any delay of 450 ms or more and prompts a waveform and topo check, because WAVi's window ends at 500 ms.
  • Added the ❓ questionable and ⧪ excess synchrony flags. One case (11-year-old male) had peak alpha marked ❓ at 7.0 Hz. Before this, only ⬓ was supported.
  • Added a performance-task module. WAVi page 3 can include Trail Making A/B (target 45–77 s and 64–124 s on the pediatric printout) and Flanker counts. All three neurologist summaries used Trail Making, and it was the deciding abnormality in one of them.
  • Started an age-range library. Ranges shift substantially with age. At 11 vs 48, the voltage range is 9–22 vs 8–18 µV and theta/beta is 1.6–3.9 vs 0.8–1.8. The library holds 2 ages so far.
  • Noted for a later module: the Synapse TBI reports add balance (BTrackS mCTSIB percentiles), a smell screen and oculomotor tracking. A future TBI-panel add-on could ingest these. Out of scope for the wellness report.
  • Confirmed the pre-test checklist. TrueBrain's own testing criteria require "well-rested and adequately hydrated", which matches the v1.1 pre-scan step.

Evidence library: 9 neurofeedback studies (the June 27 email to Chad, also in Drive)

  • Added a "Brain-training options to discuss" section that appears only when triggered: by a clinician-noted concern, a PHQ-9 score of 10 or more, theta/beta above range, F3/F4 asymmetry outside range, or age 55+ with slower processing. This follows the Project rule to use each source only when indicators call for it.
  • Covers attention (Lévesque 2006; Arns meta-analysis), depression (Xia 2024 meta-analysis, g −0.60; Choi 2011 RCT), PTSD (Nicholson 2020, remission 61% vs 33%), substance use (Scott 2005, 12-month abstinence 77% vs 44%), seizures (Sterman & Egner 2006, neurologist-directed only), autism (Kouijzer 2010) and memory in older adults (Campos da Paz 2018).
  • Grades are conservative: most of these are small trials, and the client view says plainly that these are options to discuss, not prescriptions.
